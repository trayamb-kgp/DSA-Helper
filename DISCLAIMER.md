## What the extension does, and does not do

DSA Helper runs **entirely inside your browser**. In plain terms:

- It reads the problem content **already displayed to you** on the page you are actively viewing, in your own logged-in session, and only when you ask it to (a keyboard shortcut, the toolbar button, or the right-click menu) — one problem at a time. It does not crawl, spider, bulk-collect, or run on its own.
- It **makes no network requests of its own**. Nothing it reads is sent to any server operated by this extension or its author, because there is no such server. Nothing you do with it is transmitted, logged, or shared by the extension.
- **Nothing leaves your device.** What the extension keeps — your settings and templates, a short list of recently visited problems, and the problem text of the single most recent problem you used it on (to power the options-page preview and diagnostics) — is stored **locally on your device only**, and is erased when you remove the extension. It is never uploaded anywhere. Your **code is never written to disk at all**: it is used for the one action you asked for and then discarded.

Note this is **not** the same as "stores nothing" — the extension does keep the items above locally. The accurate statement is that this information **never leaves your device**.

## ChatGPT

When you use the "Ask ChatGPT" action, the extension opens ChatGPT and places a prepared prompt into the message box. The prompt is built from the problem you are viewing and, if you have enabled it, your own code. The extension does not read or extract ChatGPT's responses. Sending the prompt, and everything that follows, is subject to OpenAI's own terms and policies, and is between you and OpenAI.
